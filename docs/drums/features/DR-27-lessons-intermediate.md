# DR-27 — Intermediate lessons: D4–D6 authored content

**Phase:** D3 · **Effort:** L (content-heavy) · **Depends on:** DR-17 (extends it), and
the D3-phase trainers its exercises open (DR-14/20/24/25/26, DR-28) · **Blocks:** the
roadmap's exit condition (D6 complete = mid-intermediate)

## Why

The back half of the package. Everything below D4 makes a beginner; D4–D6 makes the
intermediate player the user asked this roadmap to reach: ghosts, shuffle, funk, compound
meter, jazz/latin introductions, chart literacy, Moeller intro.

## What it is

~36 lessons (`src/content/drums/lessons/`), same authoring pipeline and content-test
rules as DR-17. Unit sketch (order fixed by ../research-2026-08-15.md §1):

- **D4 (12):** 16th-note fluency drills · kick permutation program (DR-15 grid, weeks-long
  arc) · cross-stick + ballad grooves · hi-hat openings on the "&" · heel-up technique
  page + speed work · shuffle deep-dive (DR-24 set) · dotted/triplet reading (DR-11 L5)
  · tier-2 rudiments start (flam tap, drags) · 16th fills · first full song etude at
  tempo (DR-26) · tempo-ladder discipline lesson (the practice-method meta-lesson) ·
  D4 checkpoint.
- **D5 (12):** ghost-note program (DR-25 arc: calibration → accent studies → funk
  grooves) · rimshot consistency · 6/8 and 12/8 feels · paradiddles around the kit ·
  gap-click program (DR-12/20 internal-clock ladder) · funk repertoire · syncopated kick
  reading (Reed applications) · dynamics in fills · 2-bar fill vocabulary · half-time
  shuffle intro · second song etude · D5 checkpoint.
- **D6 (12):** syncopation fluency (Reed-style systems on the kit) · jazz ride +
  comping intro (DR-15) · bossa + cross-stick clave · samba foot intro · Moeller intro
  (DR-18's gated page + first whip exercises) · chart reading program (DR-28) · double
  strokes at speed (open-close-open) · improvised fills (DR-14 improvise slot) ·
  transcription intro (DR-20 notate-back at full difficulty) · third song etude (jazz or
  latin) · "what's next" honesty page (advanced tier: 4-way, odd meters, where to go) ·
  D6 checkpoint = **the roadmap's exit condition**.

## Experience-gate proof

Same bar as DR-17: content test green (no dangling references), driven walk of each
checkpoint flipping its level only when criteria genuinely hold, visual pass on
representative lessons. Plus the roadmap-level proof: a D6-complete profile satisfies
every researched beginner→intermediate boundary marker (checklist in DR-16 driven
against seeded records).
